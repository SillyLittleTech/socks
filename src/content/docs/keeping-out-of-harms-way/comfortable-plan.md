A comfortable plan is a short script you can follow when something online feels intense. It works because you make the plan before pressure arrives.

## Write your rules while calm!

Choose rules that are easy to remember.

- I do not send login codes to anyone.
- I do not move money because of a chat message.
- I do not install screen sharing or remote access tools for surprise support.
- I verify urgent requests through a second channel.
- I can leave and come back later.

## Pick your second channels

Use a saved phone number, official website, bookmarked admin page, or known email thread. Do not use contact details supplied inside the urgent message itself.

## Give yourself an exit line

You do not need to debate someone who is pushing you. A plain exit line is enough:

> I am going to verify this another way before I do anything.

Then stop replying until you have checked.
