Your online presence can be quiet, loud, weird, professional, playful, anonymous, named, temporary, permanent, or some mix of all of that.

The important part is choosing on purpose.

## Decide what each space is for

- A portfolio can be stable and easy to contact from.
- A blog can be exploratory and imperfect.
- Social media can be distribution, community, or neither.
- Private notes can stay private.
- Old pages can be archived, redirected, or deleted.

You do not owe every platform the same version of yourself.
